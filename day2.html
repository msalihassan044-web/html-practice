Biryani

Biryani is one of my favorite dishes. It is a delicious rice dish made with spices, meat, potatoes, and fragrant basmati rice.
I especially enjoy its rich flavor and aroma. It is often served at family gatherings and special occasions.

STEPS OF MAKING BIRYANI

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for about 20–30 minutes.
  2. Fry sliced onions until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger, garlic, tomatoes, yogurt, and spices, then cook well.
  4. Add chicken or beef and cook until the meat is tender.
  5. Boil the soaked rice separately until it is about 70–80% cooked.
  6. Layer the rice over the cooked meat.
  7. Add fried onions, mint, coriander, and a little food color if desired.
  8. Cover the pot and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes.
  9. Gently mix the biryani and serve hot.
